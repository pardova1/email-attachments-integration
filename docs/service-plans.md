# Service plans

The paid sender application has two annual service levels.

## Individual — $4 USD/year

For non-business individual use.

## Business — $10 USD/year

For business use and expanded business functions.

Both plans use a 365-day service term. Early renewal should preserve remaining paid service time according to the existing renewal rules.

The plan controls sender entitlement and access to plan-specific functions. It must never weaken transport security or file integrity. Every successful transfer, regardless of plan, still requires **SEND → RECEIVE → ✓ VERIFIED EXACT**.

Receiving a file does not require the recipient to purchase a sender plan.

Business-specific functions should be represented as explicit entitlements rather than duplicated transfer engines, so Individual and Business users share the same secure core architecture.
