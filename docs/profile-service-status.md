# Profile service status

The Profile section displays annual service entitlement using the verified license record.

## Active
User: Valid (green indicator)
Purchase date: <verified purchase date>
Next renewal date: <license renewal date>

## Expired
User: Renewal Required (red indicator)
Purchase date: <verified purchase date>
Next renewal date: <license renewal date>
Please renew your service.

Status and dates are derived from the license/payment record and are not manually entered by the user.
