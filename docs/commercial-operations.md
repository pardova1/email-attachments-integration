# Commercial and operations policy

## Annual access
- Price: $4 USD per user for one year of access.
- No monthly subscription charge is part of the current product requirement.
- Renewal creates a new annual access term.
- Local-currency presentation and payment processing will be implemented through a replaceable payment provider.

## Maintenance
- Performance, reliability, compatibility, and security improvements may ship throughout the year.
- A major software upgrade is planned annually.
- Upgrades must never intentionally alter user media or other transferred file bytes.

## Operations reporting
- Significant and critical incidents are reported immediately.
- The Operations Agent may attempt bounded automatic recovery before or alongside reporting when safe.
- A monthly health report summarizes transfer verification, recovery activity, significant incidents, and operational status.
- File content is not included in operational reports.

## Integrity rule
A transfer is successful only after the received file is cryptographically verified as identical to the original. A failed integrity check must never be reported as successful delivery.
