# Competitive transfer intelligence

The product must not become another generic upload-and-share-link service.

The Competitive Transfer Intelligence Agent continuously studies legally/publicly available technical and product information about file-transfer applications, email attachment systems, cloud sharing, transfer plugins/extensions and emerging transport methods.

The research objective is not to copy competitors. It is to identify established patterns, gaps, compatibility requirements and opportunities for a materially different email-native experience.

## Product identity to protect

**Write normal email → use familiar attachment action → select huge video/pictures/files → bridge automatically handles eligible oversized transport behind the email integration → recipient receives the email-centered delivery experience → download → ✓ VERIFIED EXACT**

Where an email provider or operating system does not expose sufficient integration APIs, the implementation must use the closest standards-compliant integration available and must not claim invisible/native interception that the platform does not permit.

## Competitive dimensions

The agent tracks:
- whether a separate transfer website/app is required;
- whether the sender must manually create/copy a link;
- whether oversized email attachments become cloud links;
- sender and recipient installation requirements;
- transfer-size limits;
- expiration behavior;
- resumability and recovery;
- security/encryption;
- original-byte preservation and integrity verification;
- email-client integrations/extensions;
- recipient experience;
- route/storage architecture where publicly documented;
- pricing and geographic/platform availability.

## Non-copying rule

Public competitor research informs requirements and differentiation. Proprietary code, private APIs, confidential information, branding and copyrighted implementation material are not copied.

## Permanent distinction target

Original-byte preservation by itself is not sufficient differentiation. The target is the combined architecture:

**email-native workflow + huge-file bridge + intelligent independent lane + automatic recovery + fastest secure eligible routing + recipient simplicity + final cryptographic verification.**
